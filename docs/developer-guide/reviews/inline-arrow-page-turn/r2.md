  - At `ef7ca7d6e`: inline, page 1 → 2 (`start` 940 → 1880) after about 2150 presses. Full screen, page 1 → 2 after about 3100 presses. `turned` messages: 0 in every case, chromium and webkit.

Reading on with a real turn saves from the new place, so the gap only matters if the reader leaves right after extending a selection. The designed intent ("Shift+arrow extends a text selection") produces it, so the likely resolution is to reword invariant 9 ("no reader turn is made or reported"), not to change code. That call belongs to the supervisor or user under R-0.
