## Findings

### F1. On a phone in landscape the book is taller than the canvas and is never wholly visible

- `[introduced]` (`MIN_HEIGHT_PX` is in the new file). Invariant **none**. Severity **Low–Medium** (a reader has to scroll the page to see the top or the bottom of every page; full screen is the way out).
- Measured, iPhone 13 landscape (WebKit, 750x342): `main` is 238 tall, 182 px of it above the resting sheet; the book is 320 (the floor), `scrollHeight` 408. At scrollTop 0, 182 of 320 px are visible; scrolled to the end, 150. Pixel 7 landscape (Chromium): 320 vs 200 px, 183 visible at the end.
- Parent, computed rather than run: the root was `h-[60dvh]` below `md` = 205 px (a 40 px toolbar plus a 165 px book) against the same 182 px, so the book was cut by 23 px there, against 138 px now.

### F2. No fixture has sections in both writing modes, so the per-section column switch is not held

- `[introduced]`. Invariant **none** directly (a detector gap next to 6/14). Severity **Low**.
- M9 (choose the count on the first section and never switch) passes the whole suite. The switch does work: the mixed-mode run above found no wrong landing, pad page or stray turn. What it lacks is a test that fails if the switch is removed or breaks.

### F3. On the phone layout, a resize while the page is scrolled measures the stuck position of the sticky box as its top, and shrinks the book

- `[introduced]` (new file). Invariant **none**. Severity **Low**.
- `top` is `el.getBoundingClientRect().top − canvasTop`. On the phone layout `.media-detail-player` is `position: sticky`, so while scrolled, the stuck position is read as a position `scrollTop` further down, and `room` loses `scrollTop`.
- Measured at 500x1100 (WebKit, phone layout, sticky confirmed), with 1500 px of content under the book: 564 px at scrollTop 0; scrolled to 400 and a 10 px resize under the book → **324 px**, `scrollHeight` 2152→1922, so the content under the viewport jumps by 240 px. Scrolled back to the top it **stays 324** until another resize at the top restores 564.
- On iPhone 13 portrait the 320 floor hides it (0.6 × 504 < 320). It needs content under the book taller than about 40 % of the canvas, which on a phone is only a long description.

### F4. On desktop the canvas floor still applies, so the 60 % share never takes effect there

- `[introduced]` (the share is new; the floor, `FLOORED_MIMES` including `application/epub+zip`, is from the feature commit). Invariant **none**. Severity **Low** (bucket B: nothing a user sees is wrong. There are two height mechanisms where the commit says one replaced the other).
- Measured at 1440x900 with 1200 px under the book: `style.height` 468 px (the 60 % share), rendered 557 px = `.media-detail-player { min-height: max(320px, 0.7 × --canvas-h) }` with `> :first-child { flex: 1 1 auto }`. Because `room ≤ canvas`, 0.6 × room is always below the 0.7 floor on desktop, so `MIN_SHARE_OF_ROOM` has no effect there. `useFillHeight` still converges, because it subtracts `offsetHeight`, not its own value.

### F5. Inline, the overlaid button covers the top of the first line's last glyphs on horizontal pages at phone widths

- `[introduced]` (the button used to sit on its own bar). Invariant **none**. Severity **Low** (the button is `bg-bg-card/80`, it overlaps 8 px of an 18 px line, and pages are turned by swiping, not by tapping there).
- Measured in the harness at 358x472 and 390x616, in both engines (e.g. page 2 at 358x472: `t` at `x 315, y 32–50` under a box at `y 8–40`). No overlap at 784x707, none for vertical text, and none in pseudo-fullscreen, where the book begins below the band.
