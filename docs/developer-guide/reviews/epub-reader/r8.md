### F2. On a phone, the cap lets the book collapse to 0 px (and the full-screen button with it) when anything under it resizes while the page is scrolled

- `[introduced]` (the `Math.min(room, …)` cap in this commit). Invariant **none**. Severity **Medium** (the book and its only full-screen control disappear on the resting page until some later resize happens at the top; no data is lost).
- Mechanism: on the phone layout `.media-detail-player` is sticky, so `top` reads as `scrollTop` while scrolled (round-7 F3, recorded). `room = canvas − padding − top` then goes to `504 − scrollTop` on an iPhone 13, and the cap now passes it through: small, zero or negative. The parent's `Math.max(MIN_HEIGHT_PX, …)` had 320 as a lower bound, so the same reproduction ended at 324 there (round-7 F3 measurement at `c54424cd5`); 0 is unreachable in the parent by construction.
- Measured, WebKit iPhone 13, 1500 px under the book (book 320):
  - scrollTop 400, 10 px resize under the book → **104 px**; viewport −20 → 84; scrolled back to the top it **stays 84** (the formula now wants 320 but no observer fires on scroll).
  - scrollTop 504, same resize → `style.height: 0px`, frame **0 px**; back at the top, still 0. The full-screen button sits inside the frame box (`overflow-hidden`), and `elementFromPoint` at its centre is not the button. Screenshot: the page between the file bar and the sheet is blank. Removing the content under it at the top restores 472.
  - Negative room (scrollTop 800): `next` is −296, React writes `-296px`, the browser rejects it and the previous height stays. So a negative value shows as a stale height, not a collapse; exact 0..319 shows as a collapse.
- Chromium Pixel 7: the resize at scrollTop 400/504 was followed by a scroll adjustment (scrollTop 313/417) and the book ended at 320 / 320 — less severe there, but the formula still produced 279 and 175 before it.
- Not a progress write: 0 progress requests during collapse and restore, and the page shown after restore is pixel-identical to before (invariant 4 and 6 hold).
- Trigger in use: anything under the book changing size while the reader has scrolled down past the book's top — a late-loading comment list or related files, expanding a description, rotating the phone.
