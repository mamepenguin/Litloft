  For those the fix works (probe below).
- What it does not cover: a dialog that renders `fixed inset-0 z-50` in place. Inside the

  fold at `half` exactly as the portalled ones did before the fix. Reachable in the sheet on

    `ShellLayout` puts into the phone sheet (`buildTabs(true)` -> `heavySummaries` ->
    `includeIds={["detailed-summary"]}`). "Save to knowledge" from the AI detailed summary in
    the raised sheet opens it.
  - knowledge `FileLinkModal`, rendered inline by `Editor` (the `knowledge-edit` slot, which
    sits in the inspector on canvas-viewer files).
- Reproduction (temporary component-fixture arrangement, Pixel 5 project, sheet at half,
  `halfSnap` 0.4, `innerHeight` 727; both removed afterwards):
  - via `useDialogPortalTarget` + `createPortal` (real `ConfirmDialog`): panel `top 281.5,

    the sheet. A tap on its backdrop closes it and the sheet stays `half`.
  - inline `fixed inset-0 z-50 flex items-center justify-center` box inside the sheet
    (the shape of `KnowledgeSaveDialog` / `FileLinkModal`): panel `top 756, bottom 916`,
    entirely below the viewport; centre does not hit it.
- The unit test added for invariant 9 only asserts `parentElement === document.body` for a
  caller that already uses the hook; no test (unit or browser) puts a dialog on screen, so
  invariant 9 is held for the hook's callers by DOM shape only, and not at all for the
  in-place ones. This is the "sweep the pattern" case: the two in-sheet in-place dialogs
  live in addon repositories.

### G2. After the sheet is lowered, the pseudo-fullscreen video is still covered, top and bottom

- Label: `[pre-existing]` (the state the fix produces, sheet at rest + pinned frame, is
  unchanged by `5fbfc1c3`; round 1 F5 predicted the strip half by reading). Invariant broken:
  none of 1-11 names full screen; this is the stated purpose of the `CollapseSheetContext`
  effect (DESIGN.md "A player going full screen lowers the sheet, because the pinned frame is
  inside the page and under the raised sheet"; user guide "Sending the video full screen
  lowers the sheet first").
- Mechanism: `[data-sheet-snap] .media-detail-player { position: sticky; z-index: 10 }`
  applies at `peek` as well as raised (the shell sets `data-sheet-snap="peek"` on phones), so
  the frame's `fixed inset-0 z-50` is ranked at 10 in the root stacking context. The app
  `Header` (`sticky top-0 z-20`) and the resting strip (`fixed ... z-40`) are both above it.
  Lowering the sheet swaps the raised sheet for the strip; it does not lift the frame.
- Reproduction (running app, iPhone 13 emulation, `requestFullscreen` removed, native mp4
  `1Mmoob8GJ44v`, sheet at rest, player's "Full screen" button): frame rect `0..664` of
  `innerHeight 664`, class `fixed inset-0 z-50`; `elementFromPoint(195, y)`:
  y=5 and y=30 -> `HEADER.sticky top-0 z-20`; y=60 -> frame; y=332 -> frame;
  y=654 and y=624 -> `mobile-inspector-peek`; y=584 -> frame. Screenshot
  `scratchpad/r2/fs-native-rest.png`: the app header across the top of the "full screen"
  video, and the strip (file name, like, favourite, `⋮`) across the bottom, cutting the
  exit-full-screen button in half.
- The same probe with the sheet raised (this build is pre-fix, i.e. `b1aded11` behaviour):
  the sheet stays up, y=332 -> `file-action-row`, y>=584 -> sheet. So the fix turns "the
  lower 60% of the video is covered" into "the top 56px and bottom 56px+inset are covered,
  including the controls row" — an improvement, not the outcome the prose states.
- Why it matters for the trajectory question: the collapse effect answers "the raised sheet
  covers the pinned frame", but the cause is that the frame is trapped in the sticky
  player's stacking context; the strip and the header are two more surfaces with the same
  relation to it, and the effect cannot lower either.
- The unit test (`VideoPlayer.test.tsx` "lowers the file sheet when it pins the frame")
  asserts only that the callback is called; no browser case pins a frame.

## Trajectory

Round 1's change (`b1aded11`) made the sheet non-modal, added a stacking tier (`z-[25]`)
between the page chrome and the sidebar, removed the dim layer, and removed the addon slot
from the strip. This fix round does two things of different shape:

- **Removing the in-sheet dialog host is a removal.** It deletes a state (a second portal
  target) that existed only because the sheet had been modal. That part converges.
- **The collapse-on-pseudo-fullscreen effect adds a branch and a prediction**, and a new
  cross-component channel (`CollapseSheetContext`) to carry it: "a player that pins itself
  must lower the sheet". It is on the same axis as round 1's tier: how the sheet stacks
  against another surface of the page. Round 1 placed the sheet by tier; this round handles
  one surface the tier could not place (a `z-50` frame trapped under a `z-10` sticky
  ancestor) by moving the sheet out of the way instead.

So yes: this round adds a branch/prediction of the kind round 1 added. G2 and G3 show the
next round it predicts. The same trapped frame is also under the app header (`z-20`) and the
resting strip (`z-40`), which collapsing cannot move, and the YouTube `.loft` player pins
itself through the same `useFullscreen` without the effect. Fixing those one at a time
would mean another channel or branch for the strip, one for the header, and a second call
site in an addon. Each would cover one more surface the last did not anticipate. The design
is being patched at this point. The common cause is one rule: the sticky player's
`z-index: 10` under `[data-sheet-snap]` confines anything the player pins. A fix at that
level would remove the effect rather than add to it. Assigning C is the supervisor's call.
