### J1 [pre-existing on fine pointers since `3f1e6284`; introduced for a coarse-primary device with a mouse/trackpad] — a double-click whose second press lands a couple of pixels away still closes the viewer from under a hidden bar
H1's press-time cause is gone, and it was checked in the app, not only in jsdom. The setup was Playwright Chromium, 834×1194, `QDpWH-0a5M8K`, full screen, and the mouse resting at the ✕ centre until the bar was at opacity 0. The double-click was sent as raw CDP `mousePressed`/`mouseReleased` with clickCount 1 then 2, and no `mouseMoved`:
- **drift 0:** both presses hit the page, and the viewer stays open. Log: `pointerdown/up/click` on the page, twice. **H1 is fixed** for a still mouse.
- **drift 2 px** (one `mouseMoved` between the clicks, which is inside a double-click's tolerance): the log is `pointerdown/up/click` on the page, then `pointermove` on the page, then `pointerdown:Close pointerup:Close click:Close`, and **the viewer closed**. The move runs `show()` → `pointer-events: auto` is set at once (only the opacity is animated), and the second press goes to ✕. The spread toggle, the quote button and the page box are hit the same way.
- Playwright's own `mouse.dblclick` sends a `mouseMoved` to the same point first. That fires `pointermove`, and the viewer closes the same way. So any harness that moves before it clicks reproduces this.
Label: on a fine-primary device, `pointermove` was bound at `70297dd5` and at `3f1e6284` too, so this is `[pre-existing]` there (worked out from the code; the parent is not running). On a coarse-primary device with a trackpad (iPad plus keyboard case), `pointermove` was unbound at `70297dd5` and is bound now, so there it is `[introduced]`. This is the drift half of H1, which the fix does not reach. Invariant 10 is at stake only where the spread toggle is hit; closing and stealing focus are outside its wording. Bucket for the supervisor. The obvious fixes (ignore a press with `detail > 1` on the bar, or delay `pointer-events` until the bar has faded in) are a new mouse branch or a timing prediction. See the trajectory section.

### J2 [introduced] — on a coarse-primary device, a pen tap in the centre can cancel itself: pen `pointermove` now shows the chrome, and the tap's `pointerup` then toggles it
The old coarse exclusion kept every `pointermove` away on a tablet, pen included. Now only `"touch"` is filtered. A pen that moves during contact sends `pointermove` with `pointerType: "pen"` between down and up. With the bar hidden, that runs `show()`, and then the zoom hook's centre-tap `toggleControls()` hides the bar again. This is the "restores here and toggles there cancels itself" trap that the hook's own comment names.
Scope: ImageGallery, ArchiveImageViewer and the PDF viewer (`mouseSelectsText` exempts only `"mouse"`).
- An Apple Pencil with hover (M2+ iPad Pro) has already shown the bar before contact. The tap then hides it: the desktop mouse model, and arguably fine.
- A pencil without hover gets a centre tap that does nothing visible whenever it jitters.
**Not measured:** the simulator cannot produce `"pen"`. The cancellation is the same mechanism as r3's W2, where a probe test showed that show-on-down plus toggle-on-up cancels. On fine-primary pen devices (Surface) it was already so, so it is `[pre-existing]` there.
The commit message claims "a moving mouse or pen counts"; for a pen this finding is the cost of that claim. Held by no test: **X2b** (exclude `"pen"` as well as `"touch"`) is LIVE. Bucket for the supervisor. Invariant 10 names touch, not pen.

- **H2 (arrows under the text layer):** fixed, measured in the running app (Playwright Chromium, portrait 834×1194, page under the arrows):

  - Zoomed with `=` ×3 (content `scale(1.95312)`), both Previous and Next hit themselves. A real click on Previous went from 2 to 1.
  - At 1400×800 (the page not under the arrows) they hit themselves too.
- **`z-10` vs what should cover the arrows:** the arrows sit inside the dialog's own stacking context (`fixed z-[60]`), so they cannot rise above anything outside the viewer.
  - Inside, the bar is also `z-10` and earlier in the DOM, so an arrow would paint over the bar only where they overlap. Measured: the bar bottom is at 60 and the arrow top at 573 (portrait) or 376 (landscape). An overlap needs a viewport height of about 170 px or less.
  - The bar owns no popover in the PDF viewer. The quote button's floating form needs an anchor, which the full-screen viewer never passes (invariant 5), and the capture basket is portalled at `z-[70]`. So nothing that should cover the arrows is covered. X8 (dropping `z-10`) is LIVE, as jsdom can hold no stacking. Only the measurement above holds it, and no `e2e-layout` fixture exists for this viewer.
- **Touch gestures on phone/tablet: does a non-`"touch"` `pointermove` arrive?** Measured, not assumed:
  - **iOS Safari, iPad Pro 11" simulator (iOS 26.5), real UIKit touches via idb, `(pointer: coarse)` true.** Tap: `pointerover:touch pointerdown:touch pointerup:touch mousemove:- mousedown:- mouseup:- click:mouse`. A swipe: `pointerdown:touch pointermove:touch pointerup:touch` (no compatibility events). A tap with a 3 pt drift: `pointermove:touch`, then `mousemove:-` etc. The compatibility `mousemove` is a plain MouseEvent (no `pointerType`) and is not listened for. `click` reports `pointerType "mouse"` but is not listened for either.
  - **Playwright Chromium, `isMobile` plus `hasTouch`.** Tap: pointer events are all `touch`, and the compatibility event is `mousemove` (not `pointermove`). A CDP touch swipe: only `pointermove:touch`.
  - So a finger never reaches `show()`. On a touch-only device the hook now behaves as the old coarse branch did, and the centre-tap toggle that `never listens for a press` protected is safe from touch. What can fight it is a **pen** (J2), and on a tablet a **trackpad** double-click with drift (J1).
