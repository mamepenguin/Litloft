### H1 [introduced] — a mouse double- or triple-click on the page under the hidden bar lands its second click on the bar's controls. At the ✕ it closes the viewer (reproduced in the app)
Reproduced in the running app (`QDpWH-0a5M8K`, page 12, zoomed with `=` ×3 so the page lies under the bar). The mouse rested at the ✕ position (css 1108,30) until the bar hid (opacity 0, and `elementFromPoint` there hit the text layer). Then one real double-click. Event log: `pointerdown DIV` (the page), `pointermove path`, `pointerup path`, `click` on the dialog, `pointerdown path`, `pointerup path`, `click path`. **The viewer closed.**
Why: the first press runs `chrome.show()` in `onFramePointerDown`. That flips the bar to `pointer-events: auto` under the cursor, and the bar is `z-10`, above the page's text layer. The second press of the double-click lands on whatever control is there: ✕ closes, the spread toggle changes the face, the quote button adds the page to the basket, and the page box takes focus and the keyboard.
At `a0b5df5a` the frame press did not show the bar. With the mouse held still, no `pointermove` fired: none was logged between the hover and the first press. The bar stays `pointer-events: none`, so both clicks reach the page and the mouse early return makes them no-ops. The parent was **reasoned, not run**, because the containers were not rebuilt (per brief). The fix-side log shows the second press target exists only because of the first press.
Reach: the page must lie under the bar. That happens whenever the view is zoomed. At fit it happens when a portrait page is height-limited (a landscape window): the page's top edge sits under the bar, and the page box (centre) overlaps it. Triple-clicking a heading line at the top of a page is the natural trigger.
Invariant 10 names "never changes the page", and the spread toggle does change the face. Closing and focus-stealing are the same defect even where the page does not change. The new jsdom test cannot see this (no hit testing). Bucket: supervisor. I would call it A under 10's intent.

### H2 [pre-existing: arrows from `3f1e6284`; the fix's stated goal depends on it] — the on-screen arrows are under the page's text layer wherever the page lies beneath them, so a mouse (or trackpad) cannot press them
Measured in the app, viewport 1139×2055 (portrait), page filling the width, at fit and zoomed. With the bar shown, the Next page button sits at (1075,1003,48×48). `elementFromPoint` at its centre returns `DIV.react-pdf__Page__textContent textLayer` (computed `z-index: 2`), and the same holds for Previous. The arrows are `absolute` with no z-index; the bar is `z-10` and does win (✕ and the page box hit themselves).
So the commit's reason for H1's code does not hold in exactly the case it cites. The message says a trackpad on a tablet "can reach the arrows". On a portrait tablet the page fills the width, the arrows sit over it and cannot be clicked, and a mouse click there turns nothing (the mouse early return). A trackpad reader can page only from the page box. A touch tap there still pages via the edge band, so touch is unaffected.

RTL entry is on the right half for both routes (unheld: H6). A link to a page inside a pair leaves `index` mid-pair, which is now safe for the quote (G1 held) and the close hand-back (V13 in r2).
