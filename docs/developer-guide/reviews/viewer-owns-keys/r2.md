### F1 [introduced] A — the player's settings panel now takes Escape from a search or quick-note modal opened over it
09588685 moves `OverFrameSettingsPanel` to `NESTED_OVERLAY_PRIORITY` (200). The same component is the inline video player's settings panel, which lives on a page with **no viewer** and no blocker, so ⌘K (`global`, tier 0) and `n` (`quick-note`, tier 0) still open their modals while the panel is open. Those modals register their Escape at `OVERLAY_PRIORITY` (100). Before the fix the panel and the modal tied at 100 and the modal, pushed later, won; now the panel outranks the modal.

The panel's Escape has `editingOnly` undefined, so while the modal's text field has focus the panel is skipped and the modal still closes — which hides the defect in the common path. As soon as focus is on anything in the modal that is not a field (Tab to a filter/result button, a click on a button in the quick-note panel), Escape closes the *invisible* player panel behind the modal and the modal stays open; a second Escape closes the modal.

## Trajectory answer
Round 1's fix did two different things:
- **Cheat sheet**: it *removes* a second source of truth (the raw `stack.find`) so the sheet and the dispatcher read the same `orderContexts` result. Converging.
- **Panel tier**: it *adds a prediction* — "an over-frame panel is always opened on top of an overlay, and nothing is opened on top of it". That holds for the slideshow panel inside a viewer and is false for the video player's panel on a plain page (F1). The obvious round-2 repair — a tier per caller (a `priority` prop, or the player passing OVERLAY and the slideshow NESTED) — would add a branch/prediction for a second round in a row, which is the C pattern. The repair that does not extend it is to drop the move (N6 shows nothing depends on it; r1 F3 was bucket B and not user-reachable), leaving the panel to win over its frame by push order as it did at 5e148791.
