### F1 [introduced] low (B): on desktop, ‹ from a first-level folder leaves focus on `<body>`
- Difference vs `3632bdf97`, from the probe above: at `3632bdf97`, clicking ‹ at depth 1 ended with
  focus in the input. At `e03d75d67` it ends on `document.body`, because reaching the root makes
  `onUp` undefined and unmounts the focused ‹ button. At deeper levels, in browsers that focus a
  clicked button (Chrome, Firefox), focus stays on ‹.
- What a user sees: browse keys (↑/↓, Enter, →, ←/Backspace) and typing a filter are handled by the
  input's `onKeyDown`, so after a mouse ‹ they do nothing until the reader clicks the field. Esc
  still closes the modal because it is a global hotkey. › already behaves this way, since the
  entered row unmounts (r3 M1 live), so this is the accepted consequence of the user's choice
  rather than a new kind of problem.
- No R-0 invariant covers focus (1–10 checked). Recorded for the ledger, no fix implied.
