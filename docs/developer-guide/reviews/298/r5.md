## Findings

### F1 — the fix's geometry is held only by class tokens; the r4 F2 overlap can come back with every test green

`[introduced]` (the row and the rewritten position assertions are new in `adfda4a9`; at `409d007b` the row does not exist) · R-0 #7 ("fixed beside the menu button, in the menu button's box … at its top") · `frontend/src/components/AppShell.tsx:52-59`, `frontend/src/components/__tests__/MenuButton.test.tsx:90-97` · low (test gap; no user-reachable defect at this SHA)

`MenuButton.test` asserts that the row contains `fixed left-3 z-50 flex gap-2` and that its children are `[menu, toggle]`. Presence of tokens does not hold the layout those tokens produce:

- M12 gives the toggle `fixed` + `left-[60px]` while it stays the row's second child. Full suite 7123 passed. Probe: at root 20 the toggle is 60..110 over the menu's 15..65 and takes the menu's right-edge hit test; at 24, 60..120 over 18..78. This is exactly r4 F2, the defect `adfda4a9` exists to remove.
- M4 adds `flex-col`: the toggle drops below the menu (probe: top 72 at root 20). Full suite green. "At its top" breaks.

`review-workflow.md` ("What a test here cannot hold"): matching class text cannot verify a layout property, and `frontend/e2e-layout/` is where real boxes are measured. No `e2e-layout` fixture contains the menu button or the toggle (`sidebar-shell.html` has the aside, scrim and content only). A measurement of the two boxes at a raised root font size (no overlap, same top, toggle left of menu right + gap) would kill M4 and M12.
