# Notes

**Notes** in the sidebar gathers the Markdown and text files of the current drive, so you can reach them without walking the folders. It needs the [Knowledge addon](../addons/knowledge.md). The notes are ordinary files in ordinary folders.

## Finding a note

- **Find a note** searches titles and folder names, not the text inside notes. Press Enter to search; **Show more** loads more results.
- **Continue writing** shows the notes you opened most recently. It appears only when you have a [profile](profile-preferences.md).
- **Recent notes** shows the most recently changed notes, grouped into **Today**, **Past 7 days**, **Past 30 days** and **Earlier**. Adding a tag or a favorite counts as a change.

## All notes

**All notes** lists every note in the drive and lets you narrow it:

- **Folders** — choose a folder to see the notes directly in it, not in its subfolders. **All** shows every note.
- **Tags** — choose a tag. With a folder chosen, only the tags in that folder are listed. Choose the tag again, or press × on it, to clear it.
- **Search this list** and **Sort** (**Updated**, **Title**, **Created**) work within what you have chosen.

On a phone, folders, tags and sort are menus above the list. Going back from a note returns you to the same list. To move or rename folders, use the Library.

Choosing a note opens it on the usual file page, where the previous and next arrows move through its folder. Markdown notes can be edited there; `.txt` files are read-only.

## Jumping to another note

While a note is open, `Cmd/Ctrl+K` opens search limited to this drive's notes. Type part of a title or folder and press Enter on a result. To search the whole drive instead, press × on **Notes** or `Backspace` in the empty field. See [narrowed to one kind](search.md#narrowed-to-one-kind).

## Writing a new note

**New note**, at the top right, opens [Quick Note](quick-note.md) with this drive, and on **All notes** the chosen folder, already set as the destination. Nothing is created until you save.

## Web clips and connections

At the bottom of the page, **Web clip** opens the form for saving a web page as a note, along with your recent clips. **Note & file connections** shows how the notes and files in the drive link to each other, as a graph.
