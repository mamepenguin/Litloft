# Profile and preferences

Litloft has no accounts. Your profile is just a nickname, and there is no email or password to it.

## Opening the settings

Press the round button at the top right of the header: your initial once you have a nickname, or a person icon before that. The **Settings** page has:

- **Profile**: set your nickname. **Switch to a different name** changes it; **Clear profile** removes it from this device.
- **What is a profile?**: what the nickname is for.
- **Display and behaviour**:
  - **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. It applies at once.
  - **Language**: the page reloads in the new language.
  - **Sidebar order**: **Reset order** puts the sidebar's sections and items back in their default order.

## What a nickname gives you

- Resuming media where you stopped, and a watch history (**Continue watching** and **Recently Viewed** on Home).
- Posting comments, and editing or deleting your own.
- The same history on every device where you enter the same nickname.

The nickname *is* the identity: anyone who types the same nickname shares that history. To keep two people's histories apart, use different nicknames. Switching to a different name does not carry your history or comments over.

## Without a nickname

Everything else works. Tagging works too, because tags belong to the drive, not to you. You cannot post comments, and resume positions are kept only in this browser.

## Settings kept on this device

Theme, language, the player's **Autoplay** switch, the image viewer's reading direction, the slideshow interval and each folder's grid or list view are saved in the browser. They do not follow you to another device.

## Watch history

There is no button to clear all history. Remove entries one at a time from the **Continue watching** and **Recently Viewed** rows. See [comments and watch history](comments-history.md).

## Locking protected drives again

**Lock**, at the bottom of the sidebar, forgets the passwords you entered and hides the protected drives again. Clearing your profile does not lock them. See [drives and access control](drives-and-access.md).
